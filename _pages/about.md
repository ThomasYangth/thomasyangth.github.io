My name is Tian-Hua Yang (杨天骅). I am currently a fourth-year graduate student in the Department of Physics at Princeton University. My advisor is <a href="https://phy.princeton.edu/people/dmitry-abanin">Prof. Dima Abanin</a>.

My main research interest lies in the theory of many-body quantum dynamics. I am mainly interested in the problem of quantum thermalization. My <a href="/publication/SSO-2026">recent work</a> developed a theory that establishes a rigorous link between quantum thermalization and (roughly speaking) the absence of conserved quantities. I have been following up on that direction to (1) connect the state-space and operator-space viewpoints of quantum thermalization and (2) establish rigorous results about thermalization.

Recently, I have also been interested in the theory of neural networks, in particular understanding feature learning.

See a list of my publications on <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=MXF9R18AAAAJ&hl=en">Google Scholar</a>.
